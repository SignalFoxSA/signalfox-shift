How this works

1. The dashboard takes a full snapshot of your Shift data and downloads a safety backup file.

2. It wipes the slate, loads the real app below, fills it with thousands of fake staff, and times the things that used to hurt: saving, reloading, the staff list, the calendar, switching teams, exporting.

3. When it finishes — or when you hit STOP — your real data is put back exactly as it was and verified.

While a test runs, don’t open Shift in another tab. During the heaviest checks the page may freeze for a few seconds — that’s the test working. STOP lands as soon as it can breathe; closing this tab always works too, and the snapshot above will be waiting here.

Which test?

Test size

Uses the app’s own demo company — 8 staff, leave with cover, one open shift, a swap — and checks the main features one by one: build, calendar, marketplace, applying, the manager’s inbox, the staff view, exports, the calendar file and the company view.
