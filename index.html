Build your own shift

One place to set up how your shifts work: how many people are on each shift, which rotation you run, and — if none of the ready-made ones fit — build your own rotation.

Step 1 — People per shift

How many people work on each shift. Every crew gets this many people — fill the empty slots in Staff. Nobody already in a crew is ever removed.

Step 2 — Rotation

Pick one rotation at a time from the Library (ready-made plus your own).

Step 3 — Build your own rotation

None of the ready-made rotations fit? Name your pattern, type one crew’s cycle, pick the shift length — it saves into the Library and becomes pickable in Step 2.

Use D = Day, N = Night, X = 24h, O = off. Separate with commas.
